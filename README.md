Welcome to the APES CIC GitHub Repository!

This repository is dedicated to supporting the staff at the Association of Protecting Exotic Species CIC in developing innovative online websites, tools, and resources that further our mission of conserving and protecting exotic species. Here, our technical team collaborates to create, manage, and refine web projects and digital solutions that help communicate our work, raise awareness, and engage with our partners.

What You'll Find Here:
•	Source code for our websites and web applications  
•	Documentation and tutorials to assist with development and maintenance  
•	Tools and scripts to automate common tasks and improve workflow  
•	Prototypes and experimental projects aimed at expanding our digital presence

Future Plans:
We are excited to announce that in due course, we will be opening this space for public participation. This means you—and anyone interested—will soon have the opportunity to contribute ideas, report issues, or even provide code improvements. Until then, this repository serves as an internal collaboration hub for our team, and we encourage all staff members to follow the updates.

Stay Updated:
For the latest developments, features, and announcements, please follow this GitHub repository or our official channels. Your support and engagement will help drive our shared mission forward.

Thank you for being a part of our effort to protect exotic species and foster a collaborative, open-source environment that empowers conservation initiatives.

For any inquiries or feedback, please contact us at developer@apes.org.uk.

– The APES CIC Web Development Team
